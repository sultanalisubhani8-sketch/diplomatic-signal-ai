Diplomatic AI 🌍

Making World Politics Easy for Everyone

Diplomatic AI is a simple AI-powered project designed to help ordinary people understand international relations, diplomacy, wars, peace agreements, and global news in easy language.

🎯 Our Mission

To make international news understandable, accessible, and useful for everyone.

✨ Key Features

* 📰 News Updates: Help users follow important international news.
* 🌍 Simple Explanations: Explain complex global events in easy words.
* 🇵🇰 Pakistan & World: Cover Pakistan’s foreign relations and global affairs.
* 🕊️ Peace & Diplomacy: Explain negotiations, conflicts, and peace agreements.
* 🌐 Urdu & English: Make global affairs accessible to more people.
* ⚖️ Balanced Information: Distinguish verified facts from claims and opinions.

🔒 Responsible AI

Diplomatic AI aims to provide neutral, educational information. News should be checked against reliable sources, and dates and source links should be shown wherever possible. The project does not replace professional journalism or official government information.

🚀 Future Vision

Build a mobile-friendly AI assistant that helps people understand what is happening around the world, why it matters, and how it may affect their lives.

Project Category: AI for Education, News Literacy, and Global Awareness.
